What this does not prove

That every disclosure was recorded. Anyone who can read the server's vault file directly reads the password without passing through any of this, and leaves no entry behind. What this record does prove is that the entries in it have not been changed since this computer last looked — which is worth having, and is less than a promise that nobody has ever seen your password.
