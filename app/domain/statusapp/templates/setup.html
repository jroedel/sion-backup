What to leave out

Large, constantly changing, and re-creatable: browser and program caches, the Downloads folder, disk images, node_modules. Nothing in here is work. The full list is on the Settings page, where you can take anything back off it.

In gigabytes; leave it empty for no limit. One virtual machine image or disk backup can be larger than everything else put together, and it is usually a copy of something that exists elsewhere.

How often

For a machine holding work that would be expensive to redo. Costs an hour at worst.

09:00, 13:00 and 21:00.

Early afternoon is a better choice than the middle of the night: the computer is switched on, awake and on a network then.

Local times, as HH:MM, separated by commas. Whatever you choose: if the computer is asleep or switched off at that time, it backs up as soon as it is next on rather than skipping the day.
