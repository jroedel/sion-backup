One pattern per line, in restic's syntax — a folder path, or something like *.iso. Caches and downloads are worth excluding; they are large, they change constantly, and nobody has ever needed one back.

Local times, as HH:MM, separated by commas. If the computer is asleep or switched off at that time, it backs up as soon as it is next on.

Higher finishes sooner and makes the computer feel slower while it runs. 2 suits an older hard disk, 6 an SSD. 0 leaves it to restic.

16 is right for most connections. 32 does fewer round trips on a fast one. 0 leaves it to restic.
