What happens to your old backups

  1. They are kept, and readable. The old bucket keeps working until a person retires it, which may be weeks later. Nothing about a move is in a hurry.
  2. Retiring it takes away the keys, and keeps the password for ever. That password is the only thing that can read what is already in there, so it is never thrown away — and replacing the bucket is the only way this system ever replaces a password, which is the whole reason for doing this.
  3. Deleting the bucket itself is a separate day, and a person does it in the storage provider's own console. Nothing in this system is allowed to delete storage.
